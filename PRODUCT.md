# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Sports fans, international television viewers, and live streaming enthusiasts seeking instant, high-definition, ad-shielded live sports events and 24/7 TV channels across desktop and mobile devices.

## Product Purpose

NTVio Live provides a premium, zero-friction streaming experience aggregating thousands of live sports matches and 3,400+ television channels from multiple high-speed CDN and mirror backends (Titan, Falcon, Phoenix, Kobra, Raptor, DLive).

## Positioning

Unlike cluttered, ad-riddled streaming aggregators, NTVio Live operates as a studio-grade broadcast monitor interface with instant multi-CDN HLS proxying, automatic CORS/Referer bypass, zero broken iframes, and instantaneous live stream switching.

## Operating Context

- Used in desktop browser viewing (including Theater mode and full-screen TV casting) and mobile handheld viewing.
- Frequently used during live sporting events where speed of discovery, real-time score/event status, and broadcast reliability are critical.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Instant live sports match discovery filtered by server, sport, and live status.
- 24/7 television channel browsing with country filters, fast search, and pagination.
- Universal player supporting native Hls.js playback, resilient proxy fallback, picture-in-picture, and keyboard shortcuts.
- User visual constraint: Must retain **Glassmorphism** aesthetic (frosted glass blur, translucent materials, specular borders, deep dark ambient lighting).

## Brand Commitments

- Brand Name: NTVio Live
- Core Aesthetic: Studio Broadcast Monitor meets High-End Glassmorphism.
- Color Energy: Deep void obsidian canvas, luminous frosted glass panels, radiant live pulse indicators (emerald, amber, electric cyan, violet accents).

## Product Principles

1. **Broadcast Studio Reliability**: Every stream and card must provide instantaneous feedback, resilient fallbacks, and clear status indicators.
2. **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**: Translucent frosted panels with multi-stop border highlights, ambient dark glows, and layered optical depth rather than flat gray rectangles.
3. **Frictionless Discovery**: Zero dead clicks, instant search, and seamless transition between live matches and 24/7 television.
